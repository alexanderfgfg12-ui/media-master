# Media Master Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 November 2026

Media Master is designed to work without user accounts, a database, or a service operated by the developer.

## Information and permissions
- The app does not ask users to register or create an account.
- Music files are selected by the user through Android's system file picker and are played on the device.
- The link downloader requests a URL entered by the user and transfers the linked file to the save location selected by the user.
- The app does not intentionally upload selected music files to the developer or store them in a developer-operated cloud service.
- Android and third-party libraries may process technical data needed to deliver their operating-system or network functionality. Their own privacy terms apply.

## Network use
The app uses an internet connection only for a user-requested direct media-file download. A URL may disclose the user's IP address to the host serving that file. The app does not promise support for platform watch pages or protected/private media.

## Data retention and deletion
The app does not maintain a developer-operated account or database. Downloaded files are saved to the location chosen by the user and can be deleted by the user using Android's file-management tools.

## Children
Media Master is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact
This policy may be updated when app features change. A public contact email and a hosted copy of this policy must be added to the Play Store listing before publication.
